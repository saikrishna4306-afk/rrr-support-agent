title: Upper thread keeps breaking
machine_model: ALL
language: en
---
NOTE: Sample training data, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: The upper thread snaps while sewing or embroidering, or frays near the needle.

Common causes: upper thread tension too high, machine threaded incorrectly, old or low-quality thread, bent or damaged needle, thread caught on the spool or guide.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine and unplug it.
2. Raise the presser foot and re-thread the upper thread from the beginning.
3. Lower the upper tension slightly.
4. Replace the needle with a new one of the correct size.
5. Check the thread path for snags or lint.
6. If the thread still breaks, contact an RRR technician.
