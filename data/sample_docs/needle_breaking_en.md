title: Needle keeps breaking
machine_model: ALL
language: en
---
NOTE: Sample training data, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: The needle snaps during sewing or embroidery, sometimes with a loud knock.

Common causes: bent needle, needle size too thin for the fabric, fabric pulled while stitching, hoop touching the needle, loose needle clamp screw.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine and unplug it.
2. Remove the broken pieces and check that nothing is left inside the needle plate area.
3. Insert a new needle fully and tighten the clamp screw.
4. Use a needle size suited to the fabric thickness.
5. Do not pull or push the fabric while the machine is stitching.
6. If the needle breaks again, stop using the machine and contact an RRR technician.

Safety: Always switch off the machine before touching the needle area.
