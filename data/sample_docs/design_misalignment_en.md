title: Embroidery design outline does not match the fill
machine_model: ALL
language: en
---
NOTE: Sample training data, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: Gaps between the outline and the filled area, or the design looks shifted or distorted.

Common causes: fabric not hooped tight, no stabilizer under the fabric, hoop too loose, stitching speed too high, fabric or hoop touching something while the machine moves.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine.
2. Re-hoop the fabric so it is tight and flat, like a drum.
3. Add a suitable stabilizer under the fabric.
4. Reduce the stitching speed.
5. Keep the area around the hoop and the fabric clear.
6. If the design is still misaligned, contact an RRR technician.
