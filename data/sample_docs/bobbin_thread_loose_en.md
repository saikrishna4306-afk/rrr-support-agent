title: Loose loops of thread under the fabric
machine_model: ALL
language: en
---
NOTE: Sample training data, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: A messy nest of thread loops under the fabric, or loose stitches on the back of the work.

Common causes: the upper thread was threaded while the presser foot was down, so it did not seat in the tension discs; bobbin inserted in the wrong direction; lint under the bobbin case.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine and unplug it.
2. Cut and remove the tangled thread carefully.
3. Raise the presser foot and re-thread the upper thread from the beginning.
4. Remove the bobbin, check its direction and put it back correctly.
5. Clean lint from under the needle plate with a small brush.
6. If loops continue, contact an RRR technician.
