NOTE: Sample training data with an invented error code, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: The screen shows E21 and the machine does not start the design.

Meaning (sample): the machine cannot detect the embroidery hoop or the arm position.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine and wait 30 seconds.
2. Remove the hoop and attach it again until it clicks into place.
3. Check that the embroidery arm cable is firmly connected.
4. Switch the machine on again.
5. If E21 stays on the screen, do not force the machine. Contact an RRR technician.
