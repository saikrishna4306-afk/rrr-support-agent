title: Machine is skipping stitches
machine_model: ALL
language: en
---
NOTE: Sample training data, not official RRR instructions.

Symptoms: Gaps in the stitching, uneven seams, or the design has missing stitches.

Common causes: dull or bent needle, wrong needle type for the fabric, needle not pushed fully into the holder, poor-quality thread.

Fix steps:
1. Switch off the machine and unplug it.
2. Replace the needle with a new one and push it fully up into the holder.
3. Use a ballpoint needle for stretch fabric and a sharp needle for woven fabric.
4. Re-thread the upper thread and re-seat the bobbin.
5. If stitches are still skipped, the machine timing may need adjusting. Contact an RRR technician.
